# Sources and assumptions

| Item | Type | Publication and period | Accessed | Implementation use and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruitment and listening priorities | Public source | [SCAS People Strategy 2023–2026](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/SCAS-People-Strategy-2023-2026-web.pdf), pp. 12–13 | 2026-11-15 | Informs selection of vacancy, application, recruitment time and offer acceptance measures. |
| Workforce scale | Public source | [SCAS Annual Report and Accounts 2025/26](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/SCAS_Annual_Report_and_Accounts_2025-26.pdf), p. 10 | 2026-11-15 | Reports 4,400 staff. Provides scale context for the full synthetic population; no staff records are imported. |
| Operational footprint | Public source | [SCAS 999 operating and dispatch model](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/what-we-do/999-emergency/999-operating-and-dispatch-model/), from April 2026 | 2026-11-15 | Describes six sectors across Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Hampshire and Oxfordshire, served by multiple facilities. The demo models dispersed locations without copying any station names or claiming their precise count. |
| Recruitment equality context | Public source | [SCAS WRES Annual Report 2025](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/document/nhs-workforce-race-equality-standard-annual-report-2025/) | 2026-11-15 | Establishes a potential contextual reference for appointment from shortlisting. Reconfirm the reporting period and definition before displaying a benchmark. |
| Reporting context | Public source | [SCAS Performance](https://www.scas.nhs.uk/about-scas/performance/), continuous publication | 2026-11-15 | Reference for the existence of public integrated reporting. The PoC imports no report rows. |
| Locations, roles, assignments and budget lines | Modelling assumption | `interview-core` seed v1.0, July–October 2026 | — | Demo proxies. The three MVP locations do not represent an inventory of SCAS sites. |
| People, establishment WTE and dated recruitment events | Synthetic | [MVP data dictionary](data-dictionary.md), `interview-core` seed v1.0 | — | Generated for teaching scenarios. The resulting KPIs describe the synthetic data only. |
| Full reference inventory | Synthetic proxy | `full-controlled` generator 2.0 | — | 36 locations (3 interview bases and 33 coded proxy bases), 12 service areas (3 interview and 9 additional proxies) and 27 role families (3 interview and 24 additional proxies). The counts provide coverage across the modelling ranges in §7.1; they are not an internal SCAS inventory. |
| Full 36-month source history | Synthetic | `full-controlled` generator 2.1, November 2023–October 2026, seed 424400 | — | 4,400 employee records across current staff and leavers. Generated assignments, pay bands, contracts, recruitment, development, absence and listening records are realistic proxies, not claims about SCAS. Protected-characteristic values are categorical synthetic inputs for later aggregated reports. |
