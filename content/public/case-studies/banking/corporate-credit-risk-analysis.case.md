# Corporate Borrower Credit Risk and Process Analysis

## Overview

- The case was based on applied research into corporate lending and borrower assessment
  in a regional Ukrainian commercial bank.

## Context

- The analysis used historical banking and borrower data from 2014–2016.
- The analysed borrower was a small agricultural business.

## Problem

- The existing creditworthiness methodology relied primarily on standard financial
  statements.
- The analysis identified that guarantees, sureties, and other off-balance-sheet
  obligations could be insufficiently reflected in the final borrower rating.
- This created a risk of overstating the borrower's liquidity, financial stability, and
  debt-servicing capacity.

## Analysis

- The Owner reviewed the corporate lending process, the historical regulatory
  methodology, financial statements, borrower ratios, and the integrated credit rating
  calculation.
- The analysis covered profitability, liquidity, capital, working capital, financial
  stability, and debt-service indicators.
- The Owner examined the roles of lending, risk, legal, security, collateral, and
  credit-committee stakeholders in the borrower-assessment process.
- The analysis documented the existing As-Is process and identified information gaps
  between the participating functions.

## Decision

- The Owner proposed a separate assessment of guarantees, sureties, and other contingent
  obligations.
- The proposed To-Be process included legal verification, risk assessment, adjustment of
  relevant financial ratios, recalculation of the integrated score, and documented
  review by risk management.
- The Owner proposed an additional credit-summary form for recording off-balance-sheet
  obligations and their potential financial impact.
- The Owner also proposed a standard information-exchange workflow between the
  participating bank functions.

## Validation

- The proposed method was tested on historical data from a real corporate borrower.

## Results

- Including off-balance-sheet obligations changed the borrower from credit class 1 to
  class 2 in one reporting period.
- In another reporting period, the borrower changed from class 5 to class 6.
- The result demonstrated that excluding contingent obligations could produce a
  materially more optimistic credit-risk assessment.

## Limitations

- The work analytically validated the proposed change but does not prove that the bank
  implemented it in production.
- No post-implementation reduction in defaults, losses, or provisions was measured.
- The bank and borrower authorised use of the underlying figures at the time of the
  research, but the original written permission is no longer available.
- The public RAG version therefore uses anonymised organisation names and does not
  reproduce the original detailed financial statements or exact guarantee amounts.
