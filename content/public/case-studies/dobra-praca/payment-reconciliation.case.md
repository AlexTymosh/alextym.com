# Payment Reconciliation and Back-Office Automation

## Overview

- The project demonstrates The Owner's ability to analyse back-office operations, map
  unstructured payment descriptions to structured customer data, automate routine
  reconciliation, and retain human control for exceptions.

## Context

- In 2017, the Owner analysed repetitive accounting work connected to customer payments
  and the bank-client application used by Dobra Praca.

## Problem

- Employees previously had to identify payments manually, match payment descriptions
  with customer records, and mark the corresponding accounting documents as settled.
- This manual reconciliation process was repetitive, time-consuming, and vulnerable to
  mismatches.

## Analysis

- The workflow used available payment references and customer information to identify
  likely matches.

## Implementation

- The Owner created a script that mapped payment descriptions from the bank-client data
  to the corresponding customer records.
- Matched payments could be connected with the relevant accounting documents without
  repeated manual checking.
- The Owner also designed lightweight internal integrations for related insurance and
  payment-processing workflows.

## Results

- The solution reduced manual payment reconciliation and contributed to saving
  approximately 8–10 hours of accounting work per week.

## Limitations

- Exceptions and unclear payments remained available for human review.
