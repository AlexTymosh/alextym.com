# Cross-Border Practical Skills Verification

## Overview

- This case demonstrates The Owner's experience in risk analysis, stakeholder
  coordination, practical assessment design, evidence-based recruitment, and
  cross-border expectation management.

## Context

- Dobra Praca recruited skilled workers such as welders and forklift operators for
  employment in other countries.
- Skill standards, equipment, working practices, and employer expectations differed
  between countries.

## Problem

- Candidate job titles and stated experience were not always sufficient to determine
  whether their practical skills met the foreign employer's expectations.
- This created a risk that a candidate could travel abroad before the employer
  discovered that the candidate's practical ability did not match the required level.

## Analysis

- The Owner identified that document checks and conventional interviews could not
  reliably validate these practical occupations.

## Implementation

- The Owner organised practical candidate assessments in cooperation with a local
  vocational education institution.
- Candidates performed relevant practical tasks using suitable training facilities and
  equipment.
- The assessments were video-recorded with the candidate's participation.
- The recordings were provided to the prospective foreign employer for preliminary
  review.

## Validation and Decision Boundary

- The final assessment of whether the demonstrated skill level met the vacancy
  requirements remained with the employer that would make the hiring decision.

## Results

- This approach reduced dependence on subjective descriptions of experience.
- It also reduced the risk of unsuitable international placement, failed probation,
  unnecessary travel, and disagreement about the candidate's practical skill level.
- Candidates gained an opportunity to demonstrate their ability directly instead of
  relying only on a written CV or interview.
- The process created a more transparent connection between the candidate, recruitment
  company, vocational institution, and foreign employer.

## Limitations

- The assessment was a practical pre-employment evaluation and should not be described
  as a formal professional certification unless an authorised qualification was issued.
