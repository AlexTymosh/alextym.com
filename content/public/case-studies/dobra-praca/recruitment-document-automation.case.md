# Recruitment Workflow and Document Automation

## Overview

- This case demonstrates the Owner's early experience in workflow automation, document
  generation, rule-based screening, service design, data reuse, process standardisation,
  and human-in-the-loop decision-making.

## Problem

- The Owner redesigned Dobra Praca's recruitment and document-processing workflow to
  reduce repeated data entry, waiting time, manual checks, and unnecessary office
  visits.

## Analysis

- The Owner identified that completing forms on visa-centre websites consumed
  substantial employee time and introduced additional waiting.

## Implementation — Customer Data and Documents

- Customers could enter initial personal and employment information through an online
  portal before visiting the office.
- Previously entered customer data was reused across later stages instead of being typed
  repeatedly into separate documents and websites.
- He created scripts that transferred previously collected customer data into
  visa-centre forms, reducing repetitive manual entry.
- Insurance documents were generated automatically from customer and visa data.

## Implementation — Screening and Standardisation

- The Owner introduced scorecards to make candidate evaluation and workflow decisions
  more consistent.
- He created lightweight VBA-based tools for initial CV screening.
- The tools searched for target phrases, stop words, red flags, and basic matches
  between candidate information and vacancy requirements.
- These tools functioned as an early rule-based ATS-style screening layer and helped
  employees prioritise applications for further review.
- The Owner replaced long written instructions with practical flowcharts and checklists
  that reduced onboarding time for new employees.
- Employees demonstrated each required stage to the customer by following the same
  standardised workflow.

## Validation

- The automation reduced transcription work while preserving employee review before
  documents were finalised.
- It supported rapid initial filtering and escalated complex or promising cases to
  qualified specialists.
- Standard cases could move through controlled checklists and process diagrams, while
  exceptions were routed to experienced employees.

## Results

- The workflow combined customer self-service, document reuse, automated form
  preparation, rule-based screening, scorecards, checklists, and human review.
- The result was a faster and more consistent recruitment process with lower dependence
  on repetitive administrative work.

## Limitations

- Automation did not make final recruitment decisions.
